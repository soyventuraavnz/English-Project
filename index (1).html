Explanation of this job

A programmer is a highly skilled professional responsible for creating modern websites, software applications, or video games for a company. However, the size of the team required for these tasks varies significantly depending on the project's scale.

  • Video games: Creating a large-scale video game usually involves a massive team of developers. These large projects require hundreds of programmers working together to build complex digital worlds.
  • Websites: In contrast, a website can easily be built by a single individual working alone. Although one person can handle it, professional teams of one to five people sometimes manage more complex web platforms.
  • Applications: Developing a standard mobile or desktop application typically requires a structured group of 10 to 15 people to handle design, coding, and testing properly.
